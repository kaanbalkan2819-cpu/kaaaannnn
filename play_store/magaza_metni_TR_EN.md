## ENGLISH (en-US)
**App name:** World Conquest
**Short description:** Pick a country, use diplomacy and dice to conquer the world.
**Full description:**
World Conquest is a turn-based strategy game played on a 3D globe.

Choose a country, scout your neighbours, send envoys, form alliances, set your policies and fight with dice to conquer the world.

• 3D globe: zoom, rotate, tap any country
• Diplomacy: envoys, non-aggression pacts, alliances
• Dice-based battles: land, naval and missile attacks
• Manage economy, population, technology and army
• Ranked matches: Elo rating and leagues
• 2-player online mode (room code)
• Plays offline, no ads

The map and countries are for gameplay only and do not express any political view.
